Playback recovery after an interruption

Stop playback in Aura before the check. Load the test song, then press ▶ in the player below.

Waiting for audio to be chosen.

  1. Make sure music is playing, then switch to Instagram or answer a phone call.
  2. Stay on a reel for at least 30 seconds, or keep the call going for a few minutes.
  3. Leave the reel or end the call. Stay away from this page for about 10 seconds and listen for whether the music comes back.
  4. Come back here, copy the log, and note which interruption you tested and whether you heard music before you came back.

The check uses the browser's own player. It records events and playback time, and never tries to restart audio after an interruption.
